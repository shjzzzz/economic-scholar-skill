# Effect of a tax reform on firm investment
We exploit staggered adoption of the reform across counties in a difference-in-differences design.
Because two-way fixed effects can be biased under staggered timing, we use the Callaway and Sant'Anna estimator.
The event-study plot shows flat pre-trends, supporting parallel trends.
Standard errors are clustered at the county level.
Investment rises by 2.1 percentage points, about 0.15 standard deviations.
Robustness checks include placebo reforms and alternative samples.
